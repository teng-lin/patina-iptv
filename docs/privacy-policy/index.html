Patina IPTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026.

This policy applies to Patina IPTV for Android phones, tablets and TV, iPad, macOS, Windows and Linux. Some features are available only on some of these. Patina is maintained by the Patina IPTV project. For privacy questions or requests, email teng.lin@gmail.com.

Data on your device

Patina stores the playlists and program guide sources you add, including the addresses, provider credentials and connection settings you supply. It also stores downloaded channel and guide information, preferences, favorites, searches, viewing history and playback progress. If you record media, Patina stores the recordings' details, and saves the media on your device or to the location you choose where that option is available.

Patina encrypts the local catalog and imported playlist copies, including the provider credentials stored there. The catalog key is protected using Android Keystore, the Apple Keychain, Windows Credential Manager or the Linux Secret Service. On Android and iPad, Patina excludes its private data from automatic system backups. On computers, your own backup tools may copy app files. Backups you export from Patina can contain playlists, credentials, settings and history. Exported backups and recorded video files are unencrypted. Keep them in a location you trust.

Patina keeps diagnostic messages about the app on your device, including a report of its last crash and, on computers, log files. It removes addresses, credentials, tokens and storage paths from these records before keeping them. This automatic removal may not catch everything.

Connections and sharing

On Android, macOS, Windows and Linux, Patina checks GitHub for new app releases weekly by default while the app is in the foreground. You can choose Never, On launch, Daily, Weekly or Monthly in Settings, and check manually at any time. GitHub can see your IP address and the app's update request. Update requests do not include provider credentials, playlists, viewing history or device identifiers. Version and package selection happen on your device. Downloads and installation require confirmation. Builds configured for automatic installation also contact the configured authenticated release feed; macOS builds with Sparkle contact their configured appcast. Those hosts can also see your IP address.

Patina connects directly to the IPTV and program guide services you configure and to servers referenced by their content, such as servers supplying streams, subtitles or artwork. Those servers can see your IP address and the resources you request. Your provider receives the sign-in details or other identifiers needed for its service. For portal services, this can include the MAC address, serial number or device identifiers you enter, plus the portal language and time zone settings. Patina does not read your device's hardware MAC address to populate these fields. The provider and its content hosts apply their own privacy policies. Patina uses HTTPS for addresses entered without a scheme. An address that explicitly uses HTTP can send data without transport encryption.

Requests also include the app's User-Agent and connection headers you configure. Guide updates can make requests automatically, and an Android recording you start can continue requesting media while the app is in the background.

When you cast, Patina looks for receivers on your local network and sends the video to the Chromecast or AirPlay receiver you choose. Provider addresses and credentials stay in Patina; the receiver gets a temporary address on your device. Depending on the receiver and platform, the video can cross your local network without encryption, so cast only on a network you trust. If you pair with an AirPlay receiver, Patina or your device's system may store that pairing. The receiver and its maker, such as Google or Apple, apply their own privacy policies.

Send a diagnostic log in Settings creates a report only when you ask and confirm. It contains app, version and device-type details and recent diagnostic messages. You choose whether to save, share or copy it, and Patina does not upload it. The storage location or app you choose receives it.

If you export a backup or recording, the storage location or service you select can receive the file. If you choose an external player where supported, that player receives the requested media and applies its own privacy policy.

Patina has no Patina account or cloud service. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and sends no automatic diagnostics or viewing history to the Patina IPTV project. Your OS or app store may offer its own diagnostic reporting, subject to its settings and privacy policy. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and retain it for as long as needed to resolve the request or meet legal obligations.

Retention and deletion

Patina keeps app data on your device until it is cleared, replaced or removed. If your saved library is damaged and you choose to start over or restore a backup, Patina keeps the damaged encrypted copy, including its playlist copies, in its private data. It keeps up to three such copies. You can remove playlists and clear viewing and search history in the app. Clearing the app's storage on Android or deleting the app and its data from the device removes its local app data. On a computer, removing the app can leave its data folder and stored key behind; delete them as well to remove local data. Delete exported backups, diagnostic logs and recordings saved elsewhere from their storage location. Contact your IPTV provider to ask about data held by that provider.
